Malaysia Car Dashboard by gurazuru

Welcome to the interactive vehicle registration visualization project. This data science dashboard tracks and analyzes official Malaysia JPJ data, automotive insights, and Total Industry Volume (TIV) trends.

Data is sourced cleanly from the official Portal Data Terbuka Malaysia (data.gov.my) to provide comprehensive updates on vehicle trends across the country.

If you are not redirected automatically, you can access the live application directly hosted at shinyapps via gurazuru.shinyapps.io/jpj_car_viz/.
